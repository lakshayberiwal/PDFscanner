Clear Scan

Full-resolution document scans to PDF. Opens your iPhone's own Camera, so no preview-quality or app compression.

How to get a sharp scan
  1. Tap Scan and take the photo with the normal Camera (good light, hold steady, page flat).
  2. Drag the 4 corners to the page edges. Auto-detect gets close.
  3. Pick Color, Grayscale or B&W, save the page, repeat, then export the PDF.
